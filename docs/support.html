How matching works

Enigo pairs you with one match at a time. As you talk, your profile details reveal gradually — interests, then bio, then location, then finally a photo — based on how much you've actually talked, not how long you've been signed up. If no compatible person is available yet, you may be paired with an AI-generated conversational partner in the meantime; it's automatically replaced with a real person as soon as one is available. See our Terms of Service for details.

Reporting or blocking someone

Open your match and use the report option from the chat screen. Reported matches are ended immediately and that pair is permanently blocked from being matched again.

Managing your subscription

Subscriptions are billed and managed through your Apple ID (App Store) or Google account (Google Play), not directly through Enigo. To cancel or change your plan, go to your device's subscription settings.

Deleting your account

Go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, matches, and message history right away — it can't be undone.
